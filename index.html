Null Hour - Support

Null Hour is a detective game. There was a party, something happened that an adult is going to have to be told about, and your best friend is being blamed for it. You have their phone because they handed it to you. Five people were there. All five lied to you.

Every case is generated fresh, so nobody can tell you the answer.

How to play

  • Tap a line of somebody's statement. The records that can test that line drop open underneath it. Tap one and you find out whether the two things can both be true.
  • A blue number after a line means you already hold something that can test it. Start there.
  • Four of the five secrets are just embarrassing. One of them is what happened. The same move that pins somebody also clears everybody else.
  • You don't submit a name, you submit a chain - who, when, how, why, each with a piece of evidence behind it. Then their best friend takes it apart link by link.

Frequently asked

  • Does it need the internet? No. The whole game is inside the app. There is no network code in it at all, so it works in aeroplane mode.
  • Where is my data? On your device only. See the Privacy Policy. The app asks for no permissions - no camera, no microphone, no photos, no location.
  • I'm stuck. Tap Margin note at the top right. Your partner can see how far you have got and will narrow the ground for you. They will never tell you the name.
  • Can I put it down halfway? Yes. Your investigation is saved on the device; the intro offers to pick it up where you left off.
  • Is it the same case every time? No. The people, their secrets, what happened, where, and who did it are all generated per case. "New case" starts another one.
  • Languages? English, Spanish and Chinese - tap the language button at the top to switch. It follows your phone's language on first launch.
