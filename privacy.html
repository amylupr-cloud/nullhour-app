Null Hour - Privacy Policy

Effective: July 29, 2026

Null Hour is a single-player detective game. Its privacy story is short, because the app makes no network requests, and asks for no permissions.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no servers of ours. Nothing can be sent anywhere because there is nothing in the app that sends.

What stays on your device

  • Your current investigation - which statements you have tested, what you have proved, and the code that regenerates the case - is saved in the app's own local storage so you can put the game down and come back. It never leaves the device. Starting a new case discards it, and deleting the app deletes it.
  • Your language choice, which follows your phone's language until you change it.

Permissions

The app requests none. It does not use the camera, the microphone, photos, contacts, location, or health data. It uses the phone's vibration engine for feedback when a contradiction lands; that involves no data.

Network use

None. The game and all of its text ship inside the app. It runs with the radio off and behaves identically in aeroplane mode.

Children

Null Hour is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children - the app is built so that it cannot.

Changes
